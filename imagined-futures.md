一百多年前，乃至几十年前，技术的迅猛发展曾激发出无数奇妙而具体的未来设想，其中许多后来成为现实。

如今，人们认为技术进步正在加速，对未来的想象却没有随之丰富，反而越来越贫乏。许多相信 AGI 即将到来的人，一边谈论着“技术奇点”，一边却仍在反复描绘健康长寿、脑机接口、太空殖民、纳米机器人、AI 夺权之类的陈词滥调。

是人类的想象力不够了，还是宇宙指派的趣味要枯竭了呢？

---

A century ago, and even just a few decades ago, waves of technological progress inspired countless wonderful and imaginative visions of the future. Many of them eventually became reality.

Today, technological progress is widely believed to be accelerating, yet our visions of the future have not grown richer. If anything, they seem to be growing poorer. Many who believe AGI is imminent talk of a "technological singularity," only to fall back on better health and longer lives, brain–computer interfaces, space colonization, nanobots, and AI takeovers—all tired clichés.

Is human imagination running dry, or are the wonders the universe has allotted us running out?
